# Halcyon Labs Employee Handbook

Halcyon Labs is a fictional 120-person company that builds weather-forecasting software for farms. This handbook is sample content for the DocChat demo.

## Working Hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 UK time, Monday to Thursday. Outside core hours, you can organise your day however suits you, as long as your team knows when you are reachable.

Fridays are "focus Fridays": there are no internal meetings, and Slack notifications are optional.

## Remote Work

Everyone may work remotely up to four days a week. Each team meets in the Bristol office on the first Tuesday of every month for planning.

The company pays a one-off home-office allowance of £600, which can be claimed in your first three months. After that, you can claim up to £150 per year for replacement equipment.

## Leave

### Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 27 days of annual leave plus UK bank holidays. Leave increases by one day for every two years of service, up to a maximum of 32 days.

Up to five unused days can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

### Sick Leave

You do not need a doctor's note for absences of up to seven consecutive days. Let your manager know before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

### Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks at full pay. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks at full pay, which can be taken at any point in the first year.

## Expenses

Submit expense claims through Ledgerly within 30 days of the purchase. Claims over £250 need approval from your manager before the purchase.

Travel should be booked in standard class. First-class rail travel is only allowed for journeys longer than three hours.

## Learning Budget

Every employee has a learning budget of £1,200 per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Equipment

New starters choose between a 14-inch MacBook Pro and a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are refreshed every three years.
