# FieldCast Product FAQ

FieldCast is the fictional flagship product of Halcyon Labs: hyperlocal weather forecasts for farms.

## Plans and Pricing

### What plans are available?

There are three plans:

- **Seedling**: free, one field, 3-day forecasts, updated every 6 hours.
- **Grower**: £29 per month, up to 25 fields, 10-day forecasts, updated hourly, frost alerts by SMS.
- **Estate**: £149 per month, unlimited fields, 14-day forecasts, updated every 15 minutes, API access and a dedicated account manager.

Annual billing gives two months free on any paid plan.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes. The Grower and Estate plans include a 21-day free trial. No card is needed to start a trial.

## Accuracy and Data

### How accurate are the forecasts?

In 2024 field trials, FieldCast's 24-hour temperature forecasts were within 1.2°C of measured values 90% of the time. Rainfall forecasts correctly predicted whether it would rain in 87% of cases.

### Where does the data come from?

FieldCast combines national weather service models with readings from on-farm sensors. Customers on the Estate plan can connect their own weather stations through the Sensor Bridge integration.

## Integrations

### Which integrations are supported?

FieldCast integrates with John Deere Operations Center, Climate FieldView and Agworld. A REST API is available on the Estate plan, with a rate limit of 600 requests per minute.

## Account and Support

### How do I cancel?

You can cancel at any time from Settings > Billing. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, and no refunds are given for partial months.

### What are the support hours?

Support is available 07:00 to 19:00 UK time, seven days a week, by chat and email. Estate customers also get phone support and a guaranteed first response within one hour.
